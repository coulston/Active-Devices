# PID Controller

<image of PID Controller>

The PID Controller board is designed as a practical laboratory experience for students to understand how to characterize a plant, and tune a PID Controller.  Through this process you will achieve the following learning objectives:
* Analyze an opamp circuit that implements a PID controller and plant.
* Characterize a plant
  * Determine the transfer function of the plant
  * Determine the frequency reponse of the plant by building its Bode plot
  * Compute gain and phase margin of a plant by building its Nyquist plot
* Hands on tuning of a PID controller.
