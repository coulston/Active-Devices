# BJT Curve Tracer - Lab 5 The Pseudo Ramp Generator

This is the fifth and final lab in a series of junior year Electrical Enineering laboratories aimed at building a BJT curve tracer. 
In this lab you will complete the assembly of the BJT curve tracer and display the characteristic curve of a BJT on an oscilloscope display.  
As you work you way through the lab you will learn how to:
* Use a software tool to perform time domain analysis of an electronic circuit.
*	Analyze and design a circuit consisting of several building blocks.
* Assemble a circuit on a PCB.
* Use test and measurement equipment to analyze electronic circuits.

This curve tracer is based on the YouTuber w2aew video 
[#232: More Circuit Fun: Simple transistor curve tracer using Stairstep generator circuit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZOLLoa2fH24). 
The hardware for this series of labs is located in this repo, [here](https://github.com/coulston/Active-Devices/tree/main/curveTracerResources).
